Orders

How do I place an order? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Pick a bouquet, tap Add, then Checkout. Checkout has three short steps: your address, a delivery date and time slot, and payment. You need an account so you can track the order afterwards.

Can I design my own bouquet? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Yes. The bouquet builder lets you choose the flower, the number of stems, the wrapping and a message card, and shows the price as you go.

How do I track my order? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Open Your account and choose Track order. You’ll see each stage: placed, packed, out for delivery and delivered.

Can I cancel an order? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Yes, until the florist starts packing it. Use Cancel on the order in your account. Online payments are refunded to the original payment method within 3–5 working days.

Our delivery partner calls the recipient’s number. Add a note at checkout, for example “leave with security”, and we’ll follow it.

Payment

How can I pay? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Cash on delivery, or online with UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets through Razorpay.

Is paying online safe? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Yes. Payments happen on Razorpay’s secure checkout, and we never see or store your card details. Every payment is checked with Razorpay before your order is confirmed.

Bouquets

How fresh are the flowers? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Every bouquet is hand-tied on the day of delivery. If it arrives damaged or wilted, send us a photo within 24 hours and we’ll replace or refund it. See the returns policy.

Can I add a message card? {% icon 'chevron-down' 20 %}

Yes, for {{ card_price|rupees }} when you design your own bouquet. Write your message in the builder and we’ll handwrite it on the card.
