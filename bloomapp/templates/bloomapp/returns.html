↩️ Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2025  Updated

🌸 At Bloomify, we take great care in handcrafting every bouquet. As flowers are perishable items, returns are handled on a case-by-case basis with your satisfaction as our top priority.

1. Eligibility for Refund

You may be eligible for a refund or replacement in the following cases:

  • The bouquet arrived in a damaged or wilted condition
  • The wrong bouquet was delivered
  • Your order was not delivered within the promised time slot
  • You received an incomplete order (missing items)

2. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in the following situations:

  • Change of mind after the order has been dispatched
  • Flowers that wilted due to improper care after delivery
  • Delivery delays caused by incorrect address provided by the customer
  • Orders placed during peak seasons (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day) where slight delays may occur

3. How to Request a Refund

To raise a refund request:

  • Contact us within 24 hours of delivery
  • WhatsApp us at +91 94289 74532 with your Order ID and photos
  • Or email us at bloomify.help@gmail.com

4. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days
  • Online payments are refunded to the original payment method
  • COD orders are refunded via bank transfer or UPI

5. Cancellations

You can cancel your order before it is packed. Once an order is marked "Packed" or "Out for Delivery", it cannot be cancelled.

To cancel, contact us immediately via WhatsApp or the Contact Us page.

6. Contact Us

For any queries regarding returns and refunds, please reach out:

  • 📱 WhatsApp: +91 94289 74532
  • 📧 Email: bloomify.help@gmail.com
