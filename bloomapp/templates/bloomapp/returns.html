Returns and refunds

Flowers can’t be sent back, so if something is wrong we replace the bouquet or refund you. Here’s how it works.

When we replace or refund

  • The bouquet arrived damaged or wilted.
  • We delivered the wrong bouquet, or something was missing.
  • We missed your delivery slot.

When we can’t

  • You change your mind after the order has been packed.
  • The flowers wilted because of how they were looked after once delivered.
  • Delivery failed because the address or phone number given was wrong.

On very busy days, like Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day, a short delay is possible. We’ll always let you know.

How to ask for one

Within 24 hours of delivery, send your order number and a photo of the bouquet by WhatsApp to +91 94289 74532 or by email to bloomify.help@gmail.com.

How refunds are paid

  • We process approved refunds within 3–5 working days.
  • Online payments go back to the card, UPI or account you paid with.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by UPI or bank transfer.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself from your account until the florist starts packing it. After it’s marked packed or out for delivery, it can’t be cancelled.
